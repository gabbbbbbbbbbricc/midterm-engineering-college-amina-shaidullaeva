About Engineering College

Structured Like a Research Lab, Paced Like a Tech Studio.

Inspired by the sharp vision of our mascot, the Bald Eagle, Engineering College replaces fragmented semester schedules with focused 3-week modular sprints. Students master one technical subject at a time under industry mentors.

Why the Bald Eagle?

The Bald Eagle represents high-altitude perspective and engineering precision—teaching students to see both the macro architecture and the micro line of code.

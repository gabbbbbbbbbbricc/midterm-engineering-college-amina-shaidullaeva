Admissions Process

How to Join the Eagle Cohort

We evaluate applicants on analytical potential, problem-solving curiosity, and readiness for intensive modular learning.

01 / Four-Step Application Pipeline

Step 01

Online Portal

Complete the prospective student registration form and select your primary track.

Step 02

Technical Assessment

Complete a 45-minute foundational math, logic, or science problem set online.

Step 03

Faculty Interview

Discuss your projects, goals, and scholarship eligibility with an academic lead.

Step 04

Enrollment Offer

Receive your official admission decision within 10 business days and secure your seat.
